Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Versione italiana

This policy explains what personal data the Chisly app (Android and iOS) processes, why, for how long and what rights you have under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"). The Italian version prevails in case of differences.

In short
  • We use your data only to run the app: wishlists, friends, groups and gifts.
  • No ads, no profiling, no selling of data, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Chisly does not handle payments or money: in a group collection it only keeps track of shares and payments, which happen outside the app.
  • App data is stored on servers in the European Union.
  • You can delete your account from the app at any time, and your data is deleted.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the service: creating your account, showing your wishlist to your friends, running groups, invitations, proposals, reservations, votes, comments, Secret Santa and group collections, showing your payment methods to participants in a collection you organise, sending account confirmation, password recovery and email change emails. Legal basis: performance of the contract, i.e. the Terms of use you accept when signing up (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Gifts for people who do not use Chisly: processing the name of a recipient without an account. Legal basis: the legitimate interest of the group members in organising a gift together and ours in providing them with the tool (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We consider it balanced because only a name is stored, the person receives nothing from us and the data is visible only to the group members.
  • Security: preventing abuse and unauthorised access, investigating malfunctions through technical logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Support: replying to the messages you send us. Legal basis: performance of the contract or, for people without an account, our legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Legal obligations, if required by an authority. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

We do not make automated decisions that produce legal effects on you. The Secret Santa draw is random and only decides who gives a gift to whom.

4. Who sees your data in the app

  • All registered users can find you by username and see your username and profile picture.
  • Your friends (accepted friendship) also see your name, birthday, bio and the wishes you made visible to friends.
  • Members of your groups see your username and picture, the proposals you make, the comments you write and, depending on group settings, who proposed or reserved a gift and the votes once voting has closed.
  • The person receiving a gift never sees the proposals, reservations, comments, votes and collection about them.
  • People invited to a group see its name, description and type and who invited them, but not its members or proposals until they join.
  • Secret Santa: group members see status, budget, date and participants. Each person sees only their own pairing (who they give a gift to): nobody else, not even the group owner, can find out who drew whom.
  • Group collection: shares, payments and participants are visible only to the participants, the organiser and the group owner. Notices are visible only to the person who receives them.
  • Payment methods: in your profile only you see them. When you organise a collection, its participants and the group owner see only the methods you chose to show for that collection, while they take part in it. The recipient never sees them.
  • Notes and private wishes are visible only to you.

5. Data about other people

When you write a note, propose a gift or comment, you enter information about another person. Please enter only what helps choose a gift and do not write sensitive or offensive information.

Recipient without an account

A group for someone's gift can be created even if that person does not use Chisly. In this case we store only the name the members use for them: no email address, phone number, photo or anything else. Whoever creates the group must enter only the name (a first name or nickname is enough) and nothing more.

  • The person receives no emails, messages or notifications from Chisly and cannot see the group.
  • The name is visible only to the group members and is kept as long as the group exists.
  • If the person signs up to Chisly, the group owner can link the group to their profile: from then on the usual rule applies, i.e. the recipient sees neither the group nor anything about their gift.
  • If the person wants their name removed, they can write to support@chisly.app: we replace the name with "Festeggiato" (recipient) or, if they ask, delete it. We do not reveal the members' proposals or reservations to them.
  • If the recipient had an account and deletes it, the group continues for its members with the generic name "Festeggiato": we do not keep their username.

6. Filling in wishes from a link

When you paste or share a product link with Chisly, the app can fill in the title and price by reading the shop's page. The page is downloaded directly by your phone, without going through our servers: the shop receives your IP address and the technical data of an ordinary visit (for example the browser type), as if you opened the page yourself. The app sends no cookies and stores none. We only store the data you confirm in the wish (title, price, link). The shop's own privacy policy applies to its processing.

8. Retention

  • Account data and content: while your account is active, or until you delete them. When you delete your account, they are removed from the database immediately. Any technical backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Payment methods: until you remove them from your profile or delete your account.
  • Groups, invitations, proposals, comments, votes, Secret Santa and collections: as long as the group exists, or until they are deleted by someone allowed to do so. A collection, with its shares and transactions, remains even if the linked gift is deleted. A wrong transaction is not deleted: it is marked as rejected and stays in the collection history.
  • Collection notices: until you mark them as read.
  • Recipient without an account: as long as the group exists, or until the person asks for it to be removed.
  • Technical logs: for the short period set by our providers, normally no more than 30 days.
  • Emails sent by the app: sending records are kept by Brevo for the period set by its service.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to handle the request and in any case no longer than 24 months.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Your account, profile, profile photo, wishlist, notes, friendships, payment methods and notices are deleted.
  • Your reservations, votes, comments, join requests and the invitations you received are deleted.
  • The gift proposals you created are deleted, together with the reservations, votes and comments other members made on them.
  • In classic groups, gift proposals meant for you are deleted too.
  • Notes other users wrote about you are deleted.
  • You leave all groups. Groups you owned pass to the longest-standing member; groups with no other members are deleted.
  • Invitations you sent remain valid, without your name.
  • Secret Santa: you leave the draws you were part of. If nobody had revealed their pairing yet, the database redraws among the remaining participants; otherwise the Secret Santa is interrupted and the owner can redo it.
  • Group collection: your share and your payments are removed from the collection. Collections you organised pass to the group owner, who can hand them to another member; your payment methods are no longer visible to anyone. Your name no longer appears on transactions you recorded or confirmed for others.
  • Groups organised for your gift continue for their members, with the generic name "Festeggiato" (recipient).

9. Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, rectify or erase your data, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format, and object to processing based on legitimate interest (Arts. 15-21 GDPR). Much of this you can do directly in the app: edit your profile, remove payment methods, delete wishes and notes, delete your account.

People who do not use Chisly and were entered as a gift recipient have the same rights.

For anything else, write to support@chisly.app. We reply within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it) or with the authority of the EU country where you live.

10. Minimum age

Chisly is only for people aged 16 or over. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

11. Security

Communication between the app and the servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is governed by database rules that let each person see only what they are entitled to, and we regularly verify these rules with security tests. Payment links shown in a collection are only accepted in the official PayPal, Revolut and Satispay formats, so nobody can make others open a different address. No system is 100% secure: in the event of a data breach likely to put your rights at risk, we will inform you as required by the GDPR.

12. Cookies and tracking

The app uses no cookies, advertising, tracking or analytics tools. The app's emails contain no tracking pixels. This website (chisly.app) is published with GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; the site sets no cookies.

13. Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way (for example adding a new provider or a new use of data), we will let you know in the app before the changes take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
